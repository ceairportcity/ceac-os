## Literal-reference rule

The supplied references are component authorities for their designated surfaces. They are not inspiration.

A different clean, modern or premium treatment does not pass if the visible component remains materially different.

Never fabricate CEAC data to fill a reference. Preserve the treatment and use truthful CEAC data or an explicit empty state.
