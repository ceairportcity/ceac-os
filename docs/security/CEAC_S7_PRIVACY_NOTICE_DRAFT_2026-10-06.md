# CEAC OS — Privacy Notice Draft v0

Date: 6 October 2026
Status: DRAFT FOR CEAC REVIEW — NOT AN APPROVED OR PUBLISHED LEGAL NOTICE

This draft is operational source material for CEAC. It must be reviewed and approved by the appropriate CEAC/privacy/legal owner before publication. It does not claim current registration or legal compliance that has not been established.

## Who this system is for

CEAC OS is used by Christ Embassy Airport City to support staff and organisational operations, including work coordination, people administration, attendance evidence, leave, projects, communication, compliance, finance/payroll administration and technical support.

## Information the system may hold

Depending on a person's relationship with CEAC and the features used, CEAC OS may hold:

- account and contact details;
- organisational role, team and employment information;
- work, project, submission and communication records;
- attendance/work-session evidence;
- leave and permission records;
- files provided for operational purposes;
- account, access and security audit records;
- protected HR information where CEAC is authorised to keep it;
- payroll/compensation/payment information for authorised HR/payroll purposes;
- location evidence only where the attendance/work-session feature requires it.

The system must not collect data merely because the software can store it.

## How access is limited

Access is based on operational authority and need, not job title alone.

Examples:

- staff see their own information and relevant operational context;
- managers see information required to manage their authorised scope;
- Administration & HR handles protected people/HR processes;
- the Group Pastor / CEO has approved leadership authority;
- Technical Support can diagnose system/access problems but does not automatically receive salary, payroll, medical or confidential HR content.

Protected HR/payroll information uses separate restricted database/storage boundaries.

## Location and attendance evidence

Location evidence may be used to support an attendance/work-session record, reconciliation or an authorised investigation of a technical/compliance issue.

It must not be treated automatically as misconduct, absence, disciplinary guilt or a payroll deduction. Missing Start Work / End Work evidence also does not by itself prove absence or misconduct.

CEAC must approve a retention period and final notice wording for location evidence before go-live.

## Files and sensitive information

Users should place information only in the correct CEAC feature. Protected medical, HR, payroll or identity documents should not be uploaded into ordinary chats, support cases or operational work records unless that feature is specifically approved for the data class.

## Security and audit

CEAC OS records security/authority changes and consequential actions where required for accountability. Access may be withdrawn when a person's CEAC access ends, while historical work and audit attribution may remain preserved.

## Retention

Final retention periods have not yet been approved. CEAC will define retention and disposal rules by data class after the necessary HR, legal and privacy review. This draft does not invent statutory periods.

## Service providers and technical operation

CEAC OS uses technical service providers, including hosting, database/authentication/storage and deployment services. The final published notice must identify the relevant provider/processing arrangements and approved cross-border/processing wording after CEAC's review.

The Standard Institute / Standard Solution built the system and is intended to hand technical ownership to CEAC Technical Support. The final handover/processing agreement must define responsibility during and after that transition.

## Access, correction and questions

The final notice must state:

- who inside CEAC receives privacy/data requests;
- how a person can request access/correction where applicable;
- how CEAC handles account/security concerns;
- how complaints or regulatory questions are escalated.

Those contact details and formal procedures are not yet recorded in the approved product policy and must be supplied before publication.

- processing/handover agreement with the builder;
- final data-purpose statements;
- retention periods;
