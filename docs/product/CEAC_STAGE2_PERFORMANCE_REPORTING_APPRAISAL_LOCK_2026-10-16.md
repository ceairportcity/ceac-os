# CEAC OS Stage 2 — OWNER-APPROVED PERFORMANCE, REPORTING AND APPRAISAL CONTRACT
**Date:** 16 October 2026 · **Decision:** Bound to integrated Stage 2 P0–P9. **Status:** owner requirements approved for implementation; **NO production/merge/DB/Auth GO**. This document adds to and supersedes narrower earlier Stage 2 reporting assumptions, not the locked V4 visual direction or existing confidential HR release architecture. Do not create a second reporting engine or duplicate Work/Project/Appraisal rows.

## A. Governing intent, roles and audience
CEAC reporting must show what the ministry, each project, each department and **each person** achieved in an actual period, by whom, with evidence, outstanding actions and meaningful next decisions. Monthly and quarterly personal performance reporting covers employed Staff, every salaried Manager, Technical Support and Administration/HR and other covered employees. **The Group Pastor is excluded from individual employee appraisals** but may still direct ministry objectives and receive authorised Executive ministry-performance reports. Volunteers may receive proportionate project contribution reports but are not assigned employee attendance, salary or mandatory employee appraisal by inference. A Manager's personal work is assessed by a different authorised superior/independent reviewer, never by themselves.

No machine-calculated employee performance SCORE, automatic ranking, disciplinary recommendation, unapproved payroll impact or attendance-as-performance judgement. The system calculates **factual indicators**, clearly labelled recorded/self-reported/verified/accepted/disputed/missing; approved human reviewers enter structured ratings and reasons. Project output counted once even when several employees contributed. Existing HR release, employee response and project close versioned records MUST be preserved.

## B. Three related report families, one authoritative evidence model
1. **Individual Performance & Achievement Report** (month and quarter): per-person attributable assigned and self-created responsibilities, detailed dated activity/evidence, project/objective contributions, verified outcomes, work due/completed/open/overdue/returned, actual period context, personal reflections and next priorities. Employees can **search, preview, print and export their own authorised report**, including a filtered project-specific contribution report. Managers/HR see their own lawful views of the same source, not copies.
2. **Project & Department Performance Report:** initial goals and metrics, participating units/people and assignment ownership, deliverables and project objective progress, person-by-person distinct activities/contribution, verified organisational output once, dependencies, dates, issues, cost where authorised, decisions, and versioned unit/overall closure. Project leads see authorised cross-unit contribution records; one participant cannot automatically see unrelated employees' HR appraisals. Per-person optional Manager close remarks are separate confidential assessment inputs.
3. **Ministry Executive Quarterly Review & Presentation:** a leadership-ready narrative of direction→objectives→project→units→authorised individual contributions and independently verified outcome, comparative periods, risks, unresolved decisions, learning and next-quarter targets. Include reporting coverage as filing status, NOT a proxy for achieved ministry performance. Executive data may drill to valid work evidence only as role-authorised, never unrestricted HR appraisals. Distinguish planned, in-progress, achieved, missed, self-reported, verified, not-recorded and late-submitted.

Use the existing Work engine, project/objective/close revisions, report periods/reports/evidence refs, attendance, compliance and appraisal evidence/entries/cycles. Extend schema/RPCs only after an inventory proves an essential relationship, snapshot or authority is missing. Query/report one source of truth at a time; no parallel performance database.

## C. Policy cadence and responsibilities — owner decision
| Requirement | Monthly cycle | Quarterly formal cycle |
|---|---|---|
| Personal achievement/evidence summary | **Required for reporting**; generated from available records with truthful missing-data state | **Required** cumulative quarter evidence; do not triple-count monthly outputs |
| Employee reflection | Supported; encouraged | Required for completed formal review, with attributable authorised exception for non-response/absence |
| Manager written progress assessment | Required when monthly appraisal is formally submitted; no fake assessment if no review was conducted | **Required** overall narrative and evidence-linked category explanations |
| Structured Manager ratings | **OPTIONAL**; Manager can use approved rubric for a monthly review | **REQUIRED** for completed formal appraisal; N/O with reason is legitimate |
| Compliance & schedule-aware attendance | Factual separate sections | Factual separate sections + appropriate verified observations |
| HR independent publication | Existing controlled review/release on assessments where applicable | **Required** before employee publication of protected quarterly Manager appraisal |
| Leadership presentation | Not mandatory | Dedicated official event and presentation-ready authorised results |

Authorised **Executive OR Administration** can initiate quarterly review event/reporting cycle under a specific privileged capability, with HR governance for protected appraisal cases, reviewer appointments, access and independent publication. Do not grant Executive unfiltered private HR access by virtue of initiating. Quarterly has presentation/session dates and department submission deadlines; participation/response status transparent, never manufactured. Monthly/quarterly reporting periods and appraisal cycles are connected but do not have to be identical records; respect existing lifecycle and audit constraints.

## D. Human-entered appraisal ratings — never system calculated
HR approves/publishes a **versioned, role-aware five-category rubric**:
1. Delivery of responsibilities: attainment against agreed work/objectives and quality of actual outcome.
2. Quality of work: completeness, accuracy, standards and rework/approval evidence.
3. Ownership and reliability: follow-through, communication and management of deadlines/obstacles.
4. Initiative/problem-solving: appropriate proactive steps and documented problem resolution.
5. Collaboration/contribution: teamwork and real personal help with shared unit/ministry outcomes.

Each applicable category gets a **Manager-selected** rating and short specific explanation/source references. Reference scale: **1 Below expectations; 2 Partially meets; 3 Meets; 4 Exceeds; 5 Exceptional; N/O Not observed or not applicable**. Record reason for N/O, and exclude from numeric averages; no auto-generated final score. Manager also enters an independently selected **overall rating** and narrative (not a computer-average of dimensions) with strengths, challenges, improvement actions and next responsibilities. Quarterly submission cannot be complete while applicable required fields are missing; a genuine Manager absence/conflict, category N/O or employee non-response requires an attributed HR exception rather than dummy scores. HR can version the rubric going forward; changing policy cannot silently rewrite historical published reviews. Access checks, reviewer identity, employee response and audit apply at DB/RPC as well as UI.

A Manager's optional feedback **at assignment/project closure** remains optional and DOES NOT gate operational completion. When given, store per participating employee, assignment/project/period/reviewer, evidence, applicable scope and audit; it joins the later review evidence pack but is not itself auto-published as employee-facing appraisal. Manager self-review prohibited. Preserve Manager requests-release → independent HR release decision → employee sees only authorised version and can respond. HR may return for corrections, and history persists.

## E. Calculation dictionary — precise denominators; no misleading scores
- **Completed during period** = distinct eligible work-item IDs with a recorded valid completion date in period. Display self-certified versus approved/verified separately and account for reopened/returned states, audit history and cancelled/excluded outcomes.
- **Work due in period** = distinct eligible commitments whose agreed due date falls within period, including their period-end status; show scope and count. **On-time delivery** = eligible due-period commitments completed on/before the current valid version of agreed deadline and within authorised completion semantics / eligible due-period commitments, **plus numerator and denominator**, with change-of-deadline history. Exclude undated work and cancelled/invalid items with an explicit excluded-count note. Do not substitute 'percentage of all currently open work'.
- **Open/overdue/returned/waiting-on/in-review** = mutually intelligible workflow state at the report's stated as-of time; show ageing/dependency/reassignment; not every overdue item is employee fault.
- **Projects contributed to** = distinct authorised project IDs with attributable activities/deliverables during period, not all project memberships. **Individual contribution** = personally attributable action, output, evidence, outcome and time where recorded; many people may contribute to one outcome, but **unique project deliverable/measure is counted once** for organisational aggregate.
- **Objectives** = approved targets and properly measured actual values with evidence, provenance, measurement unit and review/verification status. Completing a task alone never verifies objective achievement.
- **Attendance** = compare validated Office/approved Remote/Field sessions with per-person active schedule, shift, authorised leave, holidays, corrections and exemptions; show scheduled days, confirmed attended days, validated arrivals, recorded late events and unresolved discrepancies separately. A missing clock-in or pending correction is NOT confirmed absence; unpaid/paid leave, overtime, payroll and disciplinary consequence cannot be inferred.
- **Compliance** = only applicable published policy/version and required evidence, acknowledged/unacknowledged, review state, expiry, approved exception and attributable officer observations. Observations have author, date, context, evidence, subject response/dispute and status; **unverified adverse observation is not confirmed fact or automatic score impact**.
- Evidence volumes, time online or number of tasks NEVER automatically become quality, productivity, salary or appraisal ratings. Project tasks ongoing beyond a reporting period are not falsely designated as failed; status as-of date and stage milestones remain available. Do not generate invented target/weight/denominator; 'not recorded' is not zero.

Document exact field definitions, time zone (Africa/Accra) and explicit as-of/period-end semantics in code/tests; dates, locale and approved historical changes must yield stable results. Do not compare unrelated roles or units via universal league tables.

## F. Print, export and quarterly meeting outputs
- Authorised individual/monthly and individual/project achievement reports are **printable and PDF exportable** with date range, subject, authorisation, report version/as-of timestamp, executive summary, factual work outcomes, supporting source refs, confidential-section boundaries, challenges and next actions.
- Department/project contribution reports present unit outcomes, a matrix of actual attributable individual participation and verified organisational outcomes without duplication. Protect confidential HR narrative even in generated files/print endpoints.
- Quarterly reporting has a **presentation-ready** layout (ministry overview, substantive outcomes, department contributions, issues/decisions, next-quarter objectives), suitable for authorised meeting display and PDF. An editable PPTX generator is permitted only if reliably testable and authorised; do not let it block the core factual/PDF reporting or invent an additional publishing engine.
- Formal released report/appraisal/period snapshots are immutable/versioned with attributable correction/reissue and reason; 'live view' clearly distinguished from frozen issued period. Versioning, historical snapshots, consistent data/metric denominators, link target provenance, export permissions and invalidated stale references need tests. Private HR narrative must never leak through a client-side export that ignores server scopes.

## I. Scope clarification
These owner-approved reporting rules supersede the old statement that CEAC can **never** collect any rating, but do NOT authorise machine-generated ratings, automatic ranking or employee punishment. **Quarterly structured Manager ratings REQUIRED, monthly structured ratings OPTIONAL; factual metrics never become ratings.** The user has explicitly chosen these rules and instructed committing to source; do not reopen without a conflicting later explicit decision.
