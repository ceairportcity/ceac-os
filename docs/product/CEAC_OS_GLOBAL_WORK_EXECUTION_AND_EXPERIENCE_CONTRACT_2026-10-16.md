## 1. Governing intent — what the application measures
CEAC is a ministry-execution system whose primary factual unit of accountability is each **individual person's contribution** to an assigned or self-created task, deliverable, objective, project or ministry direction. An initiative such as Healing Streams may span multiple units, Managers and employees. Each participant's calls, errands, printing coordination, vendor follow-ups, evidence and verified outcome must be attributable to that participant, with dates, sequence, next steps and blockers. Aggregate project outputs (for example 50 printed books) are **counted once**, never multiplied by participating contributors. Initiative/deliverable completion, task execution, effort and physical attendance are different records.
Do NOT invent KPI scores or infer employee effectiveness from count of tasks, time online or attendance. Formal achievement uses explicit outcome/measure/evidence/authorised reviewer; narrative feedback and human appraisal are separate.

## 2. Work creation and personal execution — ALL salaried employee roles
- Staff, Unit Heads/Managers, authorised Administration and salaried Executive have the same first-class right to do and record their **own** work, subject to employment/role scope. A Manager without a team can create, follow, complete and present their own tasks, objectives and projects as authorised. No 'switch to Staff' workaround.
- Accept work assigned by someone else, self-initiated tasks, activity logs, existing responsibilities, repeating work and project-linked deliverables. Clearly label origin: assigned / self-created / private (where appropriate). Preserve existing **Give out work** form, field structure, work types, subtasks, file evidence, instructions, review/rework and authority rules.
- Self-created work can be created **with an existing authorised project and optional objective/phase** or linked to one **later**, and safely relinked when warranted with an attributable history. Accessible project picker must be searchable; only projects the actor is permitted to contribute to. Never silently turn PRIVATE Calendar/personal notes into unit-visible organisational work.
- A task has independently recorded dated activities: planned, done, in progress, waiting/blocked; brief action, factual outcome, next action, optional linked duration, note, file/evidence, creator and time, with immutable audit on changes. Multiple actions over days remain attached to ONE parent responsibility; avoid making the manager assign each telephone call separately. Each activity belongs to one accountable person; multiple people may contribute distinct activities to a shared deliverable.
- Self-recorded activity is immediately visible to its authorised owner/operational supervisors where organisational, but **does not self-certify KPI success**. Task/deliverable submission and project-level evidence follow current authorised verification workflows; do not enforce review on each trivial activity.
- Completed, cancelled, returned, overdue, waiting-on and review states, history, follow-ups, photos/files and legitimate work inputs stay findable; preserve precise event provenance.

## 3. Attendance — starts on Overview, NEVER inside Work tab
- **Start work / End work belong in the Overview/Today home dashboard**, consistent with the accepted Staff experience. Provide equivalent session controls for every employee whose attendance is required, including all Managers; include salaried Admin/Executive as applicable. Do not place personal attendance Start in Work, even if Work shows read-only related effort.
- Start flow selects Office / Remote / authorised programme/field location, with the appropriate location/evidence capture, actual timestamp, reconciliation and stale-session controls. Do not fake geolocation, presence verification or off-site authorisation.
- Approved Remote/Hybrid/Field sessions can count as **formal attendance only when the individual's HR schedule/work arrangement or explicit permission authorises them**; unapproved off-site sessions remain recorded with truthful classification, not automatically compliant. Office code/GPS corroboration and valid manager-correction/HR-audit controls retained.
- Attendance sessions and time allocated to work activities are **linked but separate**: six attended hours cannot become twelve by logging two tasks. Task effort beyond a shift is not automatically authorised overtime; no inferred salary, discipline, lateness penalty, ranking or score. Actual scheduled days, lunch, leave, weekends and pay remain individual HR-authoritative, not generic assumptions.
- Retrospective activity can be logged with dates, claimed durations and source/evidence confidence; cannot convert ChatGPT chat-session wall-clock to verified payroll hours automatically, cannot rewrite true historic attendance. Trace later corrections and review.
- Unit-switcher changes authorised **unit workspace and permissions** only; NEVER fractures a person's single identity, attendance history, personal work, session state or employment authority. Cover multi-unit manager switching.

## 4. Projects, objectives, delegation and closure
- Unit Heads may create and administer projects within their own authorised unit, even before volunteers/staff arrive, subject to project/business approval boundaries; Staff can propose projects; cross-unit membership/participation remains separately authorised. Executive Ministry directions/portfolio align without granting broad powers to ordinary managers.
- Projects are searchable by name/ref, unit, status, dates, initiative and actor; personal work can be filtered within a project. Keep accepted Projects/Task gold-standard **record table + details drawer + collaboration + objectives + versioned close**; improve missing interactions without replacing accepted structure by generic rounded cards.
- Allow objective-linked tasks, personal targets and unit objectives; distinguish private personal goals from organisational committed targets; do not claim a self-created task creates or completes a ministry KPI.
- A delegated assignment supports owner, due dates, clear purpose, checkpoints, subordinate activities, evidence, blockers, valid submission/rework/approval and independent participant contribution.
- On **assignment closure, unit return, project reporting period and project closure**, responsible Managers may optionally add a **separate narrative contribution assessment PER employee** whom they legitimately supervised on that assignment/project. Not filing assessments DOES NOT block closure or count as a negative; show 'Not assessed', not zero. Preserve actor, employee, unit, project, work/period, relevant evidence and the evaluated person's opportunity to respond through protected HR process. Manager cannot assess own work as supervisor.
- Tie project close snapshots/participant feedback to existing manager authored appraisal evidence and Reviews & Development, without parallel score engine. **Formal appraisal narrative remains private to authorised Manager/HR until manager requests release and independent HR authorises publication**; employee reply/historical review preserved. The manager's project close narrative is not automatically an employee-visible published appraisal.

## 5. Connected information architecture: one source, different authorised perspectives
| Perspective | Must show and support | Must not show or imply |
|---|---|---|
| Individual Staff/Manager | Today attendance; my responsibilities, self-work, activity diary, actions, linked projects/objectives, evidence, accepted results, private calendar/work isolated | Other employees' private work, payroll/HR evidence, privileged approvals |
| Unit Head | Own attendance + own work, assigned team tasks, overdue/blockers, review queue, delegated work, project creation/search, unit return, per-person contribution and optional close assessments | Impersonated staff role, self-approval, unrelated units, organisation-wide HR |
| Technical Support | All above normal Manager work plus separately granted Support workspace/case authority; unit membership independent from capability grants | HR/Payroll, blanket technical admin, authority management simply by title |
| Administration / HR | Own work/attendance as applicable; rosters, authenticated attendance provenance, schedule/remote authorisation, people records, approved task/project evidence feeds, review cycle setup, evidence packs, protected employee documentation, audit/corrections | Implicit pay deductions, unapproved KPI ratings, private calendar, unapproved assessment publication |
| Finance/Payroll | Only authorised pay-preparation/payment data and validated HR inputs, with existing separation of duties | Direct task timer = payroll amount, arbitrary universal time-to-pay formula |
| Executive | Distinctive leadership home identity; ministry direction → objectives → cross-unit projects → actual verified outputs, reports by period/unit, contributions by individual where role-authorised, overdue risks, unresolved decisions, evidence/provenance drill-down; legitimate personal work/attendance as applicable | Fabricated attainment, double-counted results, appraisal release bypass, blanket confidential detail surfaced by a chart |
| Volunteer | Own authorised work/project and activity history under HR-approved activation/manager confirmation, proportionate to actual role; attendance only if legitimately rostered | Employee salary/leave entitlements, employee-wide private records |

Single underlying service/record graph feeds all panels: ministry direction → project/objective → responsibility → personal activity/evidence → verified deliverable → project close → authorised HR appraisal evidence / Executive factual reports. Enforce scope at database RLS/RPC layer, not merely UI hiding; distinguish recorded, submitted, verified, accepted, overdue, unconfirmed, no-data and not-applicable. Preserve attribution, privacy, historical records, metric denominator/period and source links. Executive may drill from outcome down through unit to project to participant to authorised evidence, never private HR material by default.
A work redesign is NOT accepted until **HR & Executive READ SURFACES actually improve**—their data views, filters, context, search, actions, empty states, explanations, verification badges, report drilldowns and relevant review permissions. It is not sufficient that Staff can now write more rows.
