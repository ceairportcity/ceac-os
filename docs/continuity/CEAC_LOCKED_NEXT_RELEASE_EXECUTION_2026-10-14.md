## Locked owner policy — operational domain

**Staff, HR, payroll**
- HR verifies the genuine 18-person starting employee roster and administers invitations/department assignments; no fabricated Auth identities. HR may approve individually restricted provisional access when appointment/NDA paperwork is pending; no privileged/sensitive entitlement by default. Existing identities retained.
- HR alone authorises effective-dated individual working schedules/office/remote/weekend duty/off-day, with manager/staff requests and history. HR must attest historical balances/schedules before leave calculations. Leave remains the already accepted **24 working days/year** for eligible permanent/contract/hybrid employees with pre-agreed accrual, prorating and quarterly visibility; volunteers have no employee leave entitlement. Do not reopen or silently substitute new accrual rules.
- Employment basis (permanent, fixed-term, independent contractor where legitimately classified) is distinct from hybrid working arrangement. HR records classification from signed individual contract; flag conflicts with office policy for HR/human review; statutory treatment remains subject to applicable professional/legal verification.
- TOR's GHS 2,000 and GHS 1,000 are **entry-level reference amounts only**, never auto-populated as an employee's actual salary. HR records actual confidential agreed compensation and effective dates. HR may choose later-payroll adjustment or separate correction payroll for backdated changes, with mandatory reason, immutable prior history and **Executive approval of every correction affecting payment**. Never silently rewrite an approved payroll.
- Ordinary payroll: HR prepares, **Executive solely gives final approval**; Finance is not a compulsory intervening approver. Every fixed-term contract renewal requires Executive approval. Probation: Manager recommends, HR decides confirmation/extension and records evidence.
- Re-onboarding: individual TOR, staff data, appointment letter, NDA; HR manages per-person status, documents and provisional access. Do not assume the 13 Oct deadline authorises auto-lockout.
- Approved Work & Office Policy: weekday office 10:00–17:00 including 13:00–14:00 lunch; Saturday/Sunday/programmes individually rostered, weekly off-day, holidays as approved. Do not infer identical schedules or calculate annual leave without authoritative HR schedules.
- Attendance/lateness: verification codes **mandatory for ordinary on-site duty**, displayed at each authorised office/programme location, short-lived, server-validated and protected against replay/abuse. Manager approves missed-code correction, HR audits afterward; maintain corrected-vs-code-verified provenance. Four **verified** lateness occurrences in a month alert Manager+HR+Compliance for documented human review (not automatic fine, penalty, ranking or disciplinary action). Distinguish approved lateness, absence, missing evidence and true exception. Overtime/additional hours approved and recorded, HR determines compensation or time-off under applicable policy; no invented rate.

**Volunteer and Technical Support**
- Volunteer: manager creates roster/assignment; HR approves activation and controls status. Volunteers are **unsalaried**, eligible only for documented, approved work-related reimbursement or transport support, not employee payroll. Manager confirms activity; HR and Finance consult jointly, either may finalise with both consultations documented and attributable history. Volunteer sees own status, contract and notices.
- Technical Support: all staff **and volunteers** may create cases. Authorised Support can handle cases, membership, delegated approvals and evidence, but **never private HR/payroll data**; limit diagnostics and evidence access, enforce revocation/expiry and case boundaries.
