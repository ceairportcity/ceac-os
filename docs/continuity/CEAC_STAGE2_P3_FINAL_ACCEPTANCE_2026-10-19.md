## Owner criteria and verified disposition

1. **Own attendance for all salaried role Overviews, including Managers.** Staff `src/screens/Home.jsx` / `src/experience-v2/staff-today/StaffTodayV2.jsx` use the established session API; Managers, Administration and Executive share `EmployeeWorkSession` with their own `me.id` and `me.org_id`. Each employee starts and ends *their own* session, independent of managing one or several units. `tests/stage2SharedPersonalSession.test.mjs` checks each role's wiring; `tests/stage2-approved-role-workspaces.spec.js`, `tests/role-routing.spec.js`, `tests/acceptance-core.spec.js` check role rendering/workflow boundaries.
2. **Location evidence: authorised Office / off-site duty work, never fabricated or background tracking.** `src/lib/session.js` rejects unrecognised places, checks `org_id` and `assignee_id` before geolocation for off-site work; office must use registered authorised location ID. Location is an explicit action, not continuous tracking. `tests/attendance-location-scope.test.mjs` and SQL `supabase/tests/attendance_phase6_integrity_gate.sql` cover location integrity and corroboration. The interface labels office/programme venue and off-site assigned responsibility rather than inventing an unverified third location category.
3. **Session persistence, duplicate prevention and uncertain network acknowledgements.** Actor + tenant scoped `openSession`; the lookup requests two rows and fails closed on duplicate sessions. Start requires a server-reconfirmed *exact* created session ID; End Work and historic close require `ended_at` readback on that exact actor/organisation-owned row; another open row cannot conceal a failed close. Conflicts and unknown reads disable further action and provide explicit retry. `tests/stage2SharedPersonalSession.test.mjs` contains 15 focused registered source cases.
4. **Stale Ghana-day sessions and explicit corrections.** Ghana/Accra day rollover recomputes while the Overview is open. A previous-day open session cannot silently count as a new day or invented overtime; continue/close flows use `reconcile_work_session` plus authoritative server recheck. Separate Staff→Manager→HR correction request/approval/audit retains the original session history and does not rewrite payroll. `tests/attendance-manager-hr-correction.test.mjs`, `tests/experience-v2-workforce-correction-approval.spec.js`, `tests/phase6-attendance-integrity.spec.js`, `supabase/tests/attendance_phase2_gate.sql`.
5. **Negative authority, schedule and privacy.** Staff may not end another actor's session; Management powers never grant ownership of another employee's personal clock; authorisation remains with existing RPC/RLS. Office codes and attendance are corroborating signals, **not payroll approval, overtime calculation or automatic performance ratings**. Operational schedules/leave are preserved by existing Workforce and attendance flows; no new server authority or production DB action is implied.
