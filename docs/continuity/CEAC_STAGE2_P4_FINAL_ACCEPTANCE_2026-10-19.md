## P4 owner contract and resolved behaviour

1. **Self-recorded and assigned Work share one factual model.** A Staff member can create attributable work, attach evidence and submit for review; authorised Manager assigns, returns for rework and approves via existing source-backed authority. The sequence is exercised by `tests/acceptance-core.spec.js` (Staff/Manager real work loop, typed Work creation, blockers, review/rework, Stage 7 work capture and project/objective hierarchy) and role-specific `tests/experience-v2-work-family.spec.js` (Staff, Manager, Administration and Executive, phone/laptop/desktop, Work detail and assignment surface). AuthZ remains enforced by original RPC/RLS and role tests; no new privileged write path.
2. **Project/objective attribution and historical evidence.** Work detail uses existing authorised project/objective relationships; rejected/returned/approved work remains attributable, never silently counted as objective completion on mere assignment, and rendered factual Work history retains the original actor, status and dates. Project-specific final acceptance belongs to P5 and is not asserted here.
3. **Private personal recurrence is truly private.** `supabase/migrations/20261015090000_147_private_personal_task_recurrence.sql` owner-only RLS, `security invoker` and idempotent materialisation. Real browser `tests/private-recurrence-browser.spec.js` covers a person's create/edit/delete, overdue visibility and cross-user negative isolation; SQL `supabase/tests/private_personal_recurrence_gate.sql`. A Manager cannot inspect or materialise another person's private series. Editing does not rewrite completed occurrence history; dismissed occurrences remain dismissed.
4. **Verified write/readback and race safety.** `tests/private-recurrence-source.test.mjs` confirms exact-count private series reads under current `org_id`/profile, stale actor response rejection, verification of update/delete/pause/resume writes against the exact current owner row, and fail-closed uncertain create (no duplicate retry). Operations don't claim success solely from a 2xx empty PostgREST acknowledgement.
5. **Recurring ministry responsibilities remain staff-visible and attributable.** `tests/stage2-recurring-work.spec.js`: real weekly Manager-authorised routine appears once on Staff future Work dates; `tests/stage2AssignedRoutines.test.mjs` covers staff assignee+unit visibility, confirmed schedule source counts, explicit missed history, no stale identity exposure and Account/Accra-day refresh. `tests/recurringResponsibilities.test.js` proves weekly multiweekday/biweekly, monthly 31st and count/end-date guardrails, no duplicate occurrence. Manager may pause/edit a ministry routine under assigned authority; private Calendar repetition is not silently converted into a team work item.
6. **Permission and UI boundaries.** Work interaction parity and role exclusions are tested by `tests/role-routing.spec.js`, `tests/experience-v2-work-family.spec.js` and the actual project/objective/office journeys. P4 does not add HR/Finance/Compliance/Technical Support powers, make calendar private tasks visible to other users, or infer productivity scores.
