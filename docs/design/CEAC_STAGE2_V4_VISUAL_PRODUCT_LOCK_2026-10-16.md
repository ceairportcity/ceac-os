This is NOT a shell-only redesign. Treat V4 as the literal master of the **light background, mostly white navigation and cards, top search/identity bar, cobalt-blue primary actions, navy hierarchy, disciplined secondary text, selected task inspector, underlined inner content navigation, searchable structured work/project records, restrained meaningful status colour, clean outline iconography, density, whitespace, micro-interactions, form/disclosure semantics and mobile behaviours**.

**ALL applicable Staff, Manager, Technical Support, Administration & HR, Executive, Finance/Payroll, Volunteer pages and nested routes/modal/drawer/form/report/error/loading/empty/mobile states must be revisited to this standard.** No legacy panel may be marked complete merely because its header, sidebar or colours changed. Every page must have clear information priority, hierarchy, truthful underlying content, correct actions, search/filter behaviour and permissions.

- **Overview:** start/end attendance HERE only for each salaried worker including Managers, Technical Support Unit Heads, HR/Admin and Executive where applicable; choose Office/authorised Remote/Field; no 'switch to staff to start' workaround, no fake pay or double-counted hours. - **Work:** focused own tasks and delegated tasks; `Add my work` and preserved `Give out work` form structure, searchable project picker/link/relink at creation/later, individual dated actions and evidence, meaningful statuses, right-hand details, completed history and optional manager review. The forms beneath these actions MUST be brought to V4 quality.
- **Projects:** truthful objective/phase/participation/contribution/close history, actual individual achievements and project outcome counted once; preserve accepted Project gold functional behaviour until an explicit owner-approved V4 visual replacement passes parity.
- **HR/Administration:** suitable V4 People, access, schedule, protected employee attendance, remote approvals, appraisal inputs and independent HR review release; full tables/forms and authorised records actually improve, not just shell.
- **Executive:** **same premium V4 visual standard** with leadership-specific information hierarchy and compatible identity accents, not a separate inconsistent prior shell; verified ministry directions → unit projects → actual outcomes → individuals and decision queues; no fabricated KPI/ranking or private HR leakage.
- **Security & other:** user-visible sign-in/TOTP setup/challenge/recovery as premium V4-consistent layouts; universal authenticator-app TOTP AAL2 policy remains binding, no Google-only requirement; Calendar's existing functionality and strengths preserved; protected Payroll/Support-case authority and all current RLS/audit unchanged.
