# Leave Owner Rules — Control

Purpose: preserve the leave rules explicitly confirmed by the product owner without inventing unresolved policy mathematics.

Confirmed request categories:
- Annual leave
- Sick leave
- Maternity leave where relevant
- Paternity leave where relevant
- Study leave

Confirmed authority:
- Employee request
- Manager approval
- Administration final approval

Confirmed Annual policy — 7 October 2026:
- 24 working days per leave year;
- 6 working days credited per completed quarter;
- a joiner receives a pro-rated credit for the portion of the completed join quarter served;
- ordinary unused Annual leave expires at year end;
- there is no routine automatic carry-forward;
- legally/contractually protected exceptions and already-approved cross-year leave must be preserved;
- Annual leave usage is counted from attributable recorded workforce schedules; CEAC must not silently assume a Monday-Friday pattern.

Implementation interpretation for the locked phrase “portion of the quarter served”:
- the pro-rated first completed-quarter credit uses calendar days served divided by calendar days in that quarter;
- the resulting credit is still denominated in working-day entitlement;
- this does not infer a pre-employment work pattern.

Still unresolved and therefore not to be invented:
- sick-leave entitlement quantity/rules;
- detailed maternity/paternity eligibility and quantities;
- study-leave quantity/rules;
- approved unpaid-leave salary treatment;
- any automatic Payroll consequence of leave or attendance.

Legacy numeric defaults in `leave_settings` are compatibility-only and are not authoritative.
