# Leave Owner Rules — Control

Purpose: preserve the leave rules explicitly confirmed by the product owner without inventing policy mathematics.

Confirmed:
- Annual leave
- Sick leave
- Maternity leave where relevant
- Paternity leave where relevant
- Study leave
- Manager review first
- Administration final approval

Unresolved and therefore not to be invented:
- entitlement quantities
- start-of-year grant vs accrual
- accrual rates
- carry-over and limits
- unpaid-leave rules
- detailed maternity/paternity/study eligibility and duration
- payroll consequences

Legacy numeric defaults in leave_settings are compatibility-only and must not become authoritative unless explicitly confirmed.
